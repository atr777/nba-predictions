02 / Method

How a pick gets made

Full methodology
07:00 ET, five runs a day

Ingest

Finished games come in from the NBA stats API, with ESPN as an independent backup.

Since 2000

Rate

Every team carries an Elo rating built from every game since 2000. Win and it rises, lose and it falls, more when it's a surprise.

Before tip-off

Lock

Each game that hasn't started gets a pick and a probability, written to the ledger with a timestamp. Live games are skipped.

After the final

Grade

The final is graded against the locked pick, and misses stay on the record.
